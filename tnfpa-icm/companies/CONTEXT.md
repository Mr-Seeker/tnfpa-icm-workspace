# companies — one folder per company (product)

Create a company by copying `_templates/company/` to `companies/<slug>/` (W01 step 1). Each company folder
has its own `CLAUDE.md` entry, its memory, models, connectors, skills, agents, domain records and outputs.
Companies never share state except through ratified priors in `portfolio/library/`.

A fully worked Tamil Nadu company is in `examples/kaveri-chairs-coimbatore/`.
