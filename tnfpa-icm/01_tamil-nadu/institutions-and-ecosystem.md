# Institutions and ecosystem (who matters to a TN finance team)

**Regulators & government:** Commercial Taxes Department, Tamil Nadu (state GST administration);
CBIC/GSTN; Income-tax Department (TDS via TRACES); EPFO and ESIC regional offices; Registrar of
Companies, Chennai and NCLT Chennai bench (schemes, insolvency); Tamil Nadu Pollution Control Board
(consents); Directorate of Industrial Safety and Health (factory licences); Labour Department; Tamil
Nadu Electricity Regulatory Commission and TNPDCL; Registration Department (stamp duty, guideline values);
TNRERA (real estate).

**Industrial development & investment:** Guidance Tamil Nadu (investment promotion and single window),
SIPCOT (industrial parks), TIDCO, TANSIDCO (MSME estates), TIIC (term lending for industry), StartupTN,
Tamil Nadu Green Climate Company, MSME Department / District Industries Centres, TN Skill Development
Corporation (Naan Mudhalvan).

**Banking & finance with Tamil Nadu roots:** Indian Bank and Indian Overseas Bank (Chennai), Tamilnad
Mercantile Bank (Thoothukudi), Karur Vysya Bank (Karur), City Union Bank (Kumbakonam), Equitas Small
Finance Bank (Chennai); NBFCs such as Cholamandalam, Shriram Finance and Sundaram Finance (Chennai);
SIDBI (MSME refinance, green finance). Registrars & transfer agents in Chennai for listed companies
include Cameo Corporate Services and Integrated Registry Management Services.

**Software built in Tamil Nadu often found in finance stacks:** Zoho (Books, Inventory, CRM, People,
Payroll, Analytics, Sheet, Creator — Chennai), Freshworks (Freshsales, Freshdesk — founded in Chennai),
Ramco Systems (ERP, HCM, payroll — Chennai), Kissflow (workflow — Chennai), Chargebee (billing — founded
in Chennai), Facilio (facilities/proptech — Chennai). Nationally common: Tally Prime, SAP, Oracle
NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics, Marg, Busy, greytHR, Keka, Darwinbox.

**Industry associations (lawful informal-intelligence sources, D29):** CII Tamil Nadu, FICCI Tamil Nadu,
Madras Chamber of Commerce & Industry, Tamil Nadu Chamber of Commerce & Industry (Madurai), CODISSIA
and SIEMA (Coimbatore), Tiruppur Exporters' Association, HOSTIA (Hosur), TANSTIA (small industries),
Tamil Nadu Spinning Mills Association, regional auditors' and CA study circles (ICAI SIRC, Chennai).
