# Reporting framework

| Topic | Rule of thumb (verify for the company) |
|---|---|
| Accounting standards | **Ind AS** for listed companies and unlisted companies with net worth ≥ ₹250 crore (and their holding/subsidiaries/associates); **AS** (Companies (Accounting Standards) Rules 2021) otherwise |
| Format | Companies Act **Schedule III** — Division I (AS) or Division II (Ind AS); amounts rounded per turnover (e.g. ₹ lakh / crore); comparative year shown |
| Schedule III disclosures that touch FP&A | ageing of trade receivables and payables (MSME separately), CWIP ageing, ratios (current ratio, debt-equity, DSCR, ROE, inventory/receivables/payables turnover, net profit ratio, ROCE) with explanation for > 25% change, title deeds, loans to promoters/KMP |
| Audit | statutory audit (Companies Act); CARO 2020 report; internal financial controls; **audit trail** in accounting software mandatory (Accounts Rules, Rule 3(1)) |
| Tax audit | s.44AB-equivalent above turnover limits; Form 3CD reconciliations (GST turnover vs books) |
| Cost records/audit | specified industries above thresholds (Companies (Cost Records and Audit) Rules) |
| Listed entities | SEBI LODR: quarterly results in 45 days (annual 60), related-party approvals, BRSR for top-1000, risk management committee for top-1000 |
| Revenue recognition | Ind AS 115 / AS 9: dealer schemes, returns and volume rebates reduce revenue; bill-and-hold needs strict conditions |
| Leases | Ind AS 116 (lease liability + right-of-use asset) vs AS 19 (operating lease rent expense). K01 uses **cash rent** in opex; D18 shows the Ind AS 116 view |
| Employee benefits | Ind AS 19 / AS 15 actuarial valuation for gratuity and leave (D16) |

**FP&A vs statutory:** the K01 engine is a management model. Its EBITDA is true EBITDA (D&A modelled);
it does not produce Schedule III statements — D03 does, from the mapped trial balance. Always label
board numbers as management (MIS) numbers unless they tie to audited statements.
