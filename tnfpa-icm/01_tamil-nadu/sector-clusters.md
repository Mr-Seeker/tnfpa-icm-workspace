# Tamil Nadu sector clusters (use to ask better intake questions)

| Cluster | Places | FP&A questions worth asking |
|---|---|---|
| Automotive & components | Chennai (Oragadam, Sriperumbudur, Maraimalai Nagar), Hosur | OEM schedule visibility, price-escalation clauses for steel/aluminium, tooling capex recovery, EV transition exposure |
| Electronics & EMS | Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Hosur, Kancheepuram | customer concentration, PLI/state incentive receivables and their timing, FX on imported components |
| Textiles & apparel | Tiruppur (knitwear export), Karur (home textiles), Erode (handloom, processing), Coimbatore & Salem (spinning) | cotton/yarn price pass-through, export incentive and duty drawback timing, buyer credit terms, labour turnover after festivals |
| Engineering & pumps/motors | Coimbatore (pumps, motors, foundries, textile machinery) | dealer network credit, agri-demand seasonality, casting input costs |
| Leather & footwear | Vellore, Ambur, Ranipet | export buyer concentration, environmental compliance costs |
| Logistics & trucking | Namakkal (trucks, poultry), Chennai & Thoothukudi ports | fleet utilisation, diesel price pass-through, driver costs |
| Agri & food processing | Cauvery delta, Erode (turmeric), Theni, Dindigul | crop-season working capital, procurement price volatility, warehousing |
| IT, ITeS & SaaS | Chennai (OMR/Guindy/Taramani), Coimbatore, Madurai | ARR/billing model (cohort skill), USD revenue hedging, utilisation |
| Renewable energy | wind belt (Tirunelveli, Thoothukudi, Coimbatore Palghat gap), solar (Ramanathapuram) | REC/green open access economics, banking charges, DISCOM receivable delays |
| Fireworks, matches, printing | Sivakasi | extreme seasonality, safety-compliance capex |
| Healthcare & medical tourism | Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Vellore | insurer/TPA receivable days, doctor fee models |
| Salt, chemicals, ports | Thoothukudi, Cuddalore, Manali | commodity pricing, port logistics |

Use this to decide which generated skills (W12) to propose: SKU profitability for product companies,
segment roll-up for multi-division groups, cohort/ARR for SaaS, fleet scorecard for logistics.
