# Dashboard: Close and variance

**Audience:** CFO, business heads  
**Refresh:** monthly  
**Sources:** fact_actuals, fact_monthly_model, D01 Versions export

## Headline tiles
- Revenue / GM / EBITDA / cash: actual vs plan and LE
- Count of material variances
- Variance by driver (volume, price, cost ratio, timing, one-time)

## Charts
- Variance bridge (waterfall) budget → actual
- Price-volume-mix bridge (D13)
- Material variance table with explanations

## Filters
period, channel, cost centre

## Rules
- Follow `../chart-standards.md`. State the unit (₹ lakh / ₹ crore) in every title.
- Stocks (cash, debt, balances) take the last period; flows sum; ratios are ratios of sums.
- Pre-close months are visually marked (hatched / lighter) and footnoted (W00).
- Before publishing, headline tiles must equal K01 › Summary / K02 › Runway for the same scenario.
