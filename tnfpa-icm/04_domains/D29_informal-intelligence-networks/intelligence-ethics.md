# Informal intelligence — rules of engagement

**Allowed:** public information; what a contact chooses to share about their *own* business; association
meetings; trade fairs; published data; dealer and customer feedback about your own products.

**Never:** unpublished price-sensitive information of a listed company (SEBI PIT); confidential competitor
pricing or bids; information obtained by pretexting, bribery or from a contact's breach of their duty;
personal data beyond what is needed.

**Grading (Admiralty-style):** reliability A (always reliable) … F (cannot be judged); credibility 1
(confirmed by other sources) … 6 (cannot be judged). D29 › Signals turns these plus corroborations into a
0–1 confidence and a route: ≥ 0.7 → context correction proposal (W08) + intake evidence; 0.4–0.7 → watch
and seek corroboration; below → note only. Unlawful/unethical → discard and do not record the content.

**Relationship map hygiene:** pseudonymous ids, topic, owner, last contact; refresh quarterly; no personal
phone numbers or private details in the workspace.
