# Governance habits that keep FP&A honest

1. Every champion promotion has a named human approver (K05 Promotion) — reviewed by the audit committee yearly.
2. Forecast accuracy (scorecard.md) is shown to the board alongside the forecast, including misses.
3. Corrections and rejected experiments are never deleted.
4. Related-party transactions are registered and approved before execution (s.188 / LODR Reg 23).
5. The vigil mechanism (s.177) reaches the audit committee chair directly; POSH Internal Committee is
   constituted and its annual report filed.
6. Accounting software audit trail is on and reviewed (Rule 3(1)).
7. CSR spend follows the board-approved annual action plan; unspent amounts move as the law requires.
8. Ethics dilemmas are logged (`ethics-log.csv`: `date,situation,options,decision,decided_by,principle`) and
   discussed without blame.
