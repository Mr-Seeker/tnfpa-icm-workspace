## Tamil Nadu notes
- Relationship and trust usually precede terms in TN business culture; plan more than one meeting and involve the right senior person.
- Seniority and family roles in promoter-led firms shape who can concede — map decision rights before the meeting, without stereotyping individuals.
- Some counterparties prefer auspicious dates for signing; respect it in the timeline rather than treating it as a tactic.
