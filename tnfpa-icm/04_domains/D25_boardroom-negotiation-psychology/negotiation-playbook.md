# Negotiation playbook (prepare → meet → debrief)

## Before the meeting (agent prepares, human owns)
1. **Define the decision** and who can say yes on each side (decision rights, not titles).
2. **BATNA** — your best alternative if no deal (another bank, another supplier, defer the capex). Put a
   utility value on it in `Deal!B5` and the evidence in a decision file. Improve it before you negotiate.
3. **Issues** — list every negotiable issue (rate, collateral, tenor, covenants, payment terms, volume,
   exclusivity, timing). Weight them (sum 100%). For each: target, walk-away, your estimate of their
   walk-away and the evidence for it.
4. **ZOPA check** — if their likely reservation is worse than yours on a high-weight issue, plan trades on
   other issues or improve your BATNA.
5. **Concession plan** — small, labelled, reciprocal steps on low-weight issues; never concede on a
   high-weight issue without getting something you value.
6. **Anchoring and framing** — open with a justified, specific first offer; frame in the counterpart's
   terms (risk for a banker, reliability for a supplier, growth for a dealer).

## In the meeting
Listen first; summarise their interests back; separate people from positions; trade, don't split the
difference; write down every agreed point before leaving.

## After
Update `Issues!H` (offer on table) → `Deal` decides accept / continue / walk; record the outcome and
lessons in `memory/decisions/`; log any prediction you made in D26 so calibration improves.

## Ethics
No deception about facts, no fabricated competing offers, no exploiting personal vulnerabilities. Cultural
context (relationship-first, seniority, family roles, auspicious timing) is something to **check and
respect**, never to assume about an individual.
