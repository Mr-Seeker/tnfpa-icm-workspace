# Judgement toolkit

- **Decision journal** — before a material decision write: the decision, the alternatives, the key
  uncertainties, what you expect to happen (with probabilities in D26 › Predictions), and what would
  change your mind. Review at the resolution date.
- **Reference class first** — start from the base rate of similar situations (last 6 festival seasons,
  last 5 monsoon years, peers in the cluster), then adjust for the specifics.
- **Pre-mortem** — "It is a year from now and this failed. Why?" List causes, rate likelihood, add the top
  three as risks (D15) or as tests in the plan.
- **Red team** — one person argues the opposite case using the same data.
- **Separate signal from story** — a vivid anecdote (D29) is one data point; corroborate before it moves a driver.
- **Calibration** — the Brier score in D26 shows whether "70% sure" means 70%. Aim for calibration, not certainty.
