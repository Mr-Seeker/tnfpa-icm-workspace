# Tamil Nadu / India judgment reflexes (add to W00)

| Trap | What to check |
|---|---|
| GST inside revenue | Tally "Sales" ledgers are usually ex-GST, but bank receipts and dealer statements are GST-inclusive. Model ex-GST; model GST cash separately (D24). |
| ITC timing | Input credit is claimable only when the supplier's invoice shows in GSTR-2B. A late-filing supplier turns ITC into a temporary cash cost. |
| Festive pull-forward | Dealers pre-buy 3–5 weeks before Deepavali and Pongal. A strong September/October is often borrowed from November/January — do not annualise it. |
| Aadi / Purattasi / Margazhi effects | Some categories slow sharply in Aadi (mid-Jul–mid-Aug, fewer weddings/home purchases) while "Aadi sales" discounting lifts volume at lower margin; Purattasi cuts non-veg demand; Margazhi shifts temple/tourism flows. |
| Monsoon and cyclones | NE monsoon (Oct–Dec) and cyclones (e.g. Vardah 2016, Michaung 2023) stop sites and logistics in Chennai and the delta for days. Treat one-off disruptions as `context` corrections, not trend. |
| Year-end push | March invoicing to hit targets often reverses as April returns/credit notes. Check credit notes before reading Q4 run-rate. |
| Bonus and statutory lumps | Bonus (often paid before Deepavali), advance tax (15 Jun/Sep/Dec/Mar), PF/ESI (15th), GST (20th), TDS (7th) are lumpy cash items — they belong in K02 as dated flows, not smoothed. |
| MSME payables | Unpaid dues to micro/small suppliers beyond 45 days are disallowed for tax (s.43B(h)) — a cash-vs-tax trade-off, not just a DPO lever. |
| Cash credit drawing power | Bank CC limits depend on stock and debtor statements (drawing power), not just the sanctioned limit. "Undrawn limit" ≠ available liquidity. |
| Promoter / related-party flows | Unsecured loans from directors and relatives, and related-party job work, can hide true margins and cash. Separate them (D28 RPT register). |
| Lakh/crore slips | A ₹ crore figure read as ₹ lakh is a 100× error. State the unit in every table header. |
| Subsidy receivables | State/PLI incentives are accrued but paid late and irregularly — never count them as near-term cash without a sanction letter and history. |
